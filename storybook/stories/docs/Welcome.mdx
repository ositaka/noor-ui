## Why This Storybook?

NoorUI has two homes:

- **[noorui.com](https://noorui.com)** — curated docs, guides, examples, and the full story behind the design system.
- **This Storybook** — the interactive sandbox. Toggle between 4 themes, RTL/LTR, light/dark in real time. Inspect every variant via Controls. Test accessibility live.

The component stories below are the main attraction. These doc pages give you just enough context to orient yourself.

## What to Do Here

1. **Use the toolbar** — switch Direction (RTL/LTR), Theme, Mode (light/dark), and Locale from the icons above.
2. **Browse components** — the sidebar lists all 74 components by category. Each has 8-15 stories with Controls.
3. **Read these docs** — Getting Started, Colors, Theming, and RTL Development cover the essentials.

For full documentation, guides, and examples, visit **[noorui.com](https://noorui.com)**.
